# About Us — HFV Protocol  

## Company Overview
HFV Protocol Technologies Limited is a legally registered entity in **England, United Kingdom**, founded in **April 2022**.  
- **Company No.**: 14067018  
- **Legal Jurisdiction**: United Kingdom corporate law  
- **Core Focus**: Development of HFV Protocol — a blockchain ecosystem for staking, governance, airdrops, treasury management, and decentralized integrations.

HFV operates under the principle of **transparent tokenomics** and **community-first governance**. All smart contracts are designed to be **on-chain, auditable, and verifiable by the community**.

---

## Mission
HFV’s mission is to:
- Build a **transparent, community-governed protocol**.  
- Provide **sustainable staking rewards** without inflationary risks.  
- Establish **DAO-driven decision-making** for treasury spending and ecosystem growth.  
- Reward early contributors and stakers through **fair airdrop distribution**.  
- Maintain **compliance and accountability** as a registered company.  

---

## Core Pillars
1. **DAO Governance** — On-chain proposals, quorum rules, weighted voting based on staking tiers.  
2. **Airdrops** — 15% of supply, distributed via Merkle proofs and vesting schedules.  
3. **Staking** — Four tiers (21 days, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months). Longer locks = stronger rewards & governance rights.  
4. **Treasury** — 18% allocation, DAO-governed, funding staking, audits, grants, and ecosystem projects.  
5. **Integrations** — Wallets, exchanges, infrastructure providers, and community platforms.
