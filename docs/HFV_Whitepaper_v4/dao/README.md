# DAO & Governance — HFV Protocol  

📅 Updated: 26 August 2025  

## Overview
HFV DAO governs all major decisions: **treasury spending, airdrop schedules, governance parameters, and integrations**. It ensures **transparent, on-chain, community-driven control**.

---

## Proposal Lifecycle
1. **Draft** — Idea proposed by community member.  
2. **Review** — Discussion period, feedback from stakers.  
3. **Vote** — On-chain voting, weighted by staking tier.  
4. **Execute** — Smart contract enforces the result.  

---

## Staking Tiers & Rights
- **Tier 1 (21 days)** → Participation rights.  
- **Tier 2 (3 months)** → Proposal review rights.  
- **Tier 3 (6 months)** → Voting rights with weight boost.  
- **Tier 4 (12 months)** → Full governance rights (submit, vote, execute).  

---

## Anti-Dump Rule
- If a user sells **>25%** of their claimed HFV post-staking, they lose DAO rights.  
- Enforced by:  
  - Snapshot of balances  
  - DAO contract check on eligibility  

---

## Transparency
- All proposals/votes are on-chain.  
- Quorum ensures decisions can’t pass without community involvement.  
- Governance history = permanent, public audit trail.
